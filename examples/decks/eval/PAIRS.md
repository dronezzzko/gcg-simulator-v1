# Evaluation panel and the held-out split

Generated by `oracle/eval_decks.py` from the deck files; do not edit. The same split is in
`docs/dev/eval/panel.json`, which `gcg-eval` reads (`--decks panel --pairs tuning|held-out`).

**Pre-registration.** This split is committed before any tuning run (PLAN §12.5, X16).
Every pair that contains a held-out deck is a held-out pair: it is never used in tuning,
screening or budget curves, and `gcg-eval` refuses it under `--role tuning|dev|confirm`.
The other pairs are tuning pairs. The digest of each deck pins its contents, so editing a
panel deck makes `uv run python oracle/eval_decks.py --check` fail.

Game plans:

- `blue-white-federation`: early Guntanks and Guncannons rest or ping small enemy Units, Amuro-linked Gundams take over the mid game, and white bounce/AP-reduction commands clear the way for attacks.
- `purple-white-tekkadan`: damage your own Units on purpose (CGS Mobile Worker, Ryusei-Go, Barbatos Adapt) to switch on the Barbatos line's "while damaged" bonuses, pair Mikazuki for removal, and use Gjallarhorn blockers plus white AP reduction to survive the counterattack.
- `red-green-zeon`: cheap Zakus and Zee Zulus pressure Shields from turn 2, Breach Units (Rick Dom, M'Quve-paired Zeon Units) convert trades into Shield damage, and burn commands remove blockers so Kshatriya and Sinanju can close the game.
- `blue-green-ramp`: Re-GZ BWS, AGE-1 Normal, G-Self, AGE-2 Normal and Nu Gundam add EX Resources while they develop the board, so the deck reaches Lv.7 early and casts AGE-1 Titus (Lv.7, cost 4, Breach 3) and Penelope (Middle Form); Diva pumps green Earth Federation Units at Lv.7, and blue Blockers, rest effects and Signs of a Revolution hold the early game.
- `blue-red-tempo`: a low curve of Lv.3-4 Units that link with the Biological CPU Pilots and attack at once; Chaos Gundam grants High-Maneuver, Chaos (MA Mode) has Breach 3, and cheap bounce and rest effects plus Girty Lue keep blockers out of the way.
- `green-white-link`: every Unit links with its named Pilot (Suletta Mercury with the Aerials, Elan Ceres with the Pharacts) or with any Academy Pilot, so paired Units attack on the turn they land; Covert Operative and The Witch and the Bride bounce blockers.
- `purple-blue-swarm`: flood the board with cheap Marine Units, GOOhN and Orga's Order tokens, then pair Char Aznable to give every Marine AP+1 for a wide attack; Zock and Abyss block, while Char's Z'Gok and Poorly Planned Offensive clear the way.
- `red-purple-control`: Geara Doga and Gyunei Guss (Blocker while Neo Zeon) hold the ground early, red damage Commands and purple destroy Commands remove every threat, and the Sazabis and Alpha Azieru (Blocker, draws when your Units are destroyed by effects) close the game late.

## Held-out decks

Held out: `blue-red-tempo`, `green-white-link`.

Rule (PLAN §12.5): evaluation decks with copy overlap <= 10/50 with every tuning deck and every AI test deck, sorted by (maximum copy overlap, total copy overlap, maximum name overlap, total name overlap, name) over the other twelve decks; the first 2 are held out.

The twelve decks are the other seven panel decks, the tuning decks
(`src/gcg_sim/ai/tuning.py:89-93 TUNING_DECKS`) and the AI test decks (`tests/ai/decks.py DECKS`). Copy overlap counts
main-deck copies of the same card number (W-20); name overlap counts copies with the same
card name, which also covers other printings of the same Unit or Pilot.
