GUNDAM CARD GAME WEEKEND BETA BATTLE

The Gundam Card Game Is Almost Here!

To celebrate the upcoming launch in July, we’re hosting exclusive events at select stores with strong card game communities. These gatherings are a great chance for players to meet, battle, and share their excitement before the official release.

Participating stores have been carefully selected based on their active player base and demonstrated enthusiasm for the title. Event kits and participation prizes will be distributed in limited quantities to support these gatherings.
Please note: Beta deck sets will not be distributed at the events.

Check the schedule below and get ready to turn up the hype!
