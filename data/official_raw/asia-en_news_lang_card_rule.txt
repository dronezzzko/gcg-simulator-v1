Regarding Cards in Each Language Version:

The GUNDAM CARD GAME includes three language versions of cards: Japanese, English, and Simplified Chinese. The regions where each version can be used are listed below.
However, regarding Resource and Token (including Unit Token, EX Base, EX Resource) cards, there are no regional restrictions and any language card may be used in any region.

Japanese Version – Usable Regions

- Japan

- Hong Kong S.A.R.

- Taiwan area

- South Korea

- Singapore

- Malaysia

- Indonesia

- Philippines

- Thailand

English Version – Usable Regions

- North America

- Latin America

- Europe

- Oceania

- Hong Kong S.A.R.

- Taiwan area

- Singapore

- Malaysia

- Indonesia

- Philippines

- Thailand

Note: In Hong Kong S.A.R., Taiwan area, Singapore, Malaysia, Indonesia, the Philippines, and Thailand, decks may be built using both Japanese and English version cards."
Simplified Chinese Version – Usable Region

- Mainland China

Detail above is the latest information as of July 11, 2025.
Information is subject to change.
